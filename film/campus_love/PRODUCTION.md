# CAMPUS LOVE — Episode 01: 那年夏天，她坐在我前面

あの夏、君は僕の前の席にいた。

60 seconds · 720×1280 · 12 shots · Japanese voice-over · simplified Chinese subtitles

Photorealistic Japanese live-action coming-of-age film. Nostalgic summer afternoon, natural skin texture, restrained warm grade, softly desaturated highlights, soft shadows, 35mm composition, gentle movement. No anime, idol styling, plastic skin, flashy effects, celebrity likeness or generated lettering.

## Continuity decision

Shot 11 is a brief flashback to Aoi preparing her reply, minutes before shot 10. Shot 12 returns to present. Her reply was tucked into the notebook returned to Haruto. Summer eraser stays plain white; navy umbrella appears only in shots 7–8. Maintain front/back classroom seating and same left-side windows.
