# The Seat Beside You

Five days before graduation, a shy student mistakes a class invitation for a confession—and almost misses her own first love.

## Characters

### Jiwoo

Han Ji-woo, an 18-year-old Korean final-year high-school student; warm brown eyes, straight brows, softly defined jaw, dark espresso curtain hair with a neat low taper. Navy school blazer, ivory Oxford shirt, loosened forest-green tie, charcoal trousers, white low-top sneakers; canvas backpack on right shoulder.

### Seoah

Yoon Seo-ah, an 18-year-old Korean final-year high-school student; bright dark-brown almond eyes, oval face, small beauty mark below left eye, long dark-chestnut hair with airy bangs, half-up ivory ribbon. Navy blazer over ivory blouse, forest-green ribbon tie, charcoal pleated midi skirt, white sneakers; pale-blue sketchbook.

## Visual direction

Photorealistic Korean youth romance film, natural skin texture, soft spring daylight, pastel highlights, subtle 35mm film grain, shallow depth of field, restrained acting, vertical 9:16 composition. Fictional people, no celebrity likeness.
